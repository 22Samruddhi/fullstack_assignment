About Our College

KBT College of Engineering is located on Gangapur Road, Nashik. The institute provides undergraduate and postgraduate engineering education.

The college focuses on technical education, innovation, practical learning and overall development of students.


Vision and Mission

Vision

To develop competent professionals with technical knowledge, innovation and social responsibility.

Mission

To provide quality education and encourage innovation, research and professional development.
